Bernardo Moura

Bernardo Moura is a guitarist and acoustic guitar player from Rio de Janeiro. A longtime companion of the Rio scene, he joined Rodrigo Rodriguez in the studio for the recording of Linha do Tempo.

His acoustic guitar runs through "Se Você Acha", one of the special guest moments of the album, adding a warm, timbral counterpoint to the electric guitars of the session.
