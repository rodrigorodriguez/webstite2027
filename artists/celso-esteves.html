Celso Esteves

Celso Esteves, renowned Brazilian lyricist, stood out as a multifaceted artist, leaving an indelible mark on the music scene through his emotional and poetic lyrics. His passion for Maria Bethânia and Chico Buarque inspired memorable compositions, such as “Perdão” and “Ventania”. The perfect composing partner for Celso was Roberto Ferreira, whose collaboration deeply enriched the lyricist's creations.
