Felipe Moura

Felipe Moura is a bass player from Rio de Janeiro. He joined the sessions of Linha do Tempo as one of the album's special guests.

His bass carries "O Que Você Era Pra Mim", where his low end dialogues with the drums of Ygor Helbourn and the guitars of Rodrigo Rodriguez.
