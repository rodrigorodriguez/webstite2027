Guilherme Salgueiro

Gui Salgueiro has played the night scene since he was 12 years old, starting his career at a festa junina in the neighborhood where he was born and raised: Vila Isabel. Since then he has played the most varied styles, from samba to rock, accompanying renowned artists such as Monarco, Moacir Luz, Zé Catimba, Ana Costa, Ronaldo Barcelos, Dudu Nobre and Alcione. He was part of Mart'nália's band and recorded the DVD Mart'nália em Samba with her.
