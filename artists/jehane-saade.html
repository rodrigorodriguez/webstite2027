Jehane Saade

Singer, actress, dancer and entrepreneur born in Rio de Janeiro to a Lebanese mother and a Brazilian father, Jehane Saade spent part of her childhood in Lebanon and brought the Arab world into her music — a unique blend of MPB, bossa nova, indie and world music. Entirely self-written, her album Exótica (2016, Universal Music) closes with the French-language song "Je Ne Veux Plus". Her first recorded song was a rendition of Michael Jackson's "Black or White" on a compilation with other artists. She collaborates with Rodrigo Rodriguez.
