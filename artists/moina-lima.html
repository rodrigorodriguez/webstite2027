Moína Lima

Moína Lima is a singer, composer and producer from Rio de Janeiro, daughter of the composer Taiguara. She leads Moína Produções Artísticas, an office dedicated to music production and to the memory of Brazilian song.

With Rodrigo Rodriguez she recorded Do Brasil (2010), a celebration of the Brazilian songbook. She also took part in the artistic production of Regra Zero, shaping the album's sound and arrangements.
