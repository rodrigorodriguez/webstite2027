Rodrigo Cosenza

Rodrigo Cosenza is a guitarist and composer. He took part in bands of the Rio rock scene of the late 1990s and early 2000s, such as Gioconda and Columbia. He also played in the band Perdidos em Marte, which accompanied singer and composer Cíntia Luando.
