Rodrigo Garcia

Musician and longtime collaborator of Rodrigo Rodriguez.

Rodrigo Garcia played in LogX with Rodrigo Rodriguez between 1998 and 2000.

He played in the bands Onno, Ramirez, Polar, Anna Élle, Monotube and Indievíduos, and in cover bands of Brazilian Rock, Classic Rock and MPB.

He now plays in the band alongside Gui Salgueiro.
