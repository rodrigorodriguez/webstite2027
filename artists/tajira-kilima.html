Tajira Kilima

Tajira Kilima is a singer and performer, daughter of the composer Taiguara, alongside her sister Moína Lima. Music has been her language since childhood, on stage and in the studio.

She took part in the artistic production of Regra Zero, bringing her voice and sensibility to the album's arrangements, and performs in tributes to her father's work, including Taiguara: 80 Outubros.
