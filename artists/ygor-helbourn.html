Ygor Helbourn

Born into a musical universe, Ygor Helbourn is an eclectic drummer who stamps his personality on his musical projects. For over 10 years he has been part of the band Laranjeletric, which develops a black music work rooted in blues. He has taken part in several projects and accompanied numerous national and international artists such as Marina Elali, Aki Kumar, Rodrigo Quik and Glaucus Linx.
