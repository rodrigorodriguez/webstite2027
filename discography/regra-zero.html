Regra Zero

2005

Bringing the world of Rodrigo Rodriguez's songs (composer, guitarist and vocalist), the Regra Zero songs take the listener to a particular world. In this CD, Rodriguez draws on his fight for original music and shows that it is possible to go against the tide.
