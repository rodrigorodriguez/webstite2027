Once a Microsoft fanboy, now a believer in open sovereignty

Two decades were spent deep in the Microsoft stack: .NET, SharePoint, Azure, Office 365, certifications on the wall. It taught the craft. Then AI arrived and the question changed: who owns the intelligence an organization runs on? General Bots is the answer: open source, self-hosted, AGPL-3.0, and radically cheaper to run.
